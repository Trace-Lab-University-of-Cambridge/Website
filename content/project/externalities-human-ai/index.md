<div class="project-intro">
<strong>Externalis.ai</strong> is a large-scale, multi-domain study and open-source platform to track the longitudinal effects of AI use on productivity, confidence, and equity. We conduct focused evaluations within single domains and cross-cultural settings to understand heterogeneous impacts of using AI assistance.
</div>
