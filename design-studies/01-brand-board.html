The colour and identity system for TRACE Lab.

A trustworthy-AI research lab presented like scholarship, not a product launch: confident type, hairline structure, and a disciplined palette built on the wordmark's deep blue and coral. This board is rendered in the system it proposes.

Colour

A restrained palette that does structural work

Neutral paper and deep TRACE blue carry the page; coral is a single signal — never a rainbow of category tags. Every text pairing meets WCAG AA contrast.

Why coral is rationed. On the current site coral competes with eight tag colours and animated gradients, so it reads as noise. Given one job — the logo tick, the active nav marker, a key figure — it becomes the lab's signature. Pure coral fails as small text on white, so --coral-ink carries any coral wording.
Typography

Three voices: scholarship, clarity, data

A serif display for authority, a workhorse sans for reading, and a mono reserved for real data — publication years, tags, counts. Continuous with the PI's page, elevated to a lab brand.

Principles

What the rebrand keeps and drops

The wordmark, colours, and content are honoured; the decoration that signals nothing is removed.

+ Keep & build

  • Deep blue + coral, developed into a disciplined token system
  • Structure drawn with hairline rules and whitespace
  • Serif display for scholarship; mono for real data only
  • Cambridge worn as credential — lockup, footer, affiliations
  • WCAG AA contrast on every text pairing

− Retire

  • Glassmorphism blur cards and animated gradient heroes
  • Gradient / shimmer headline text
  • Scattered ○ □ ◇ △ ▽ symbol patterns
  • The eight-colour category tag rainbow
  • Pill CTAs with heavy coloured glow
