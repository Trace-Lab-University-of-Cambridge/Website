What makes this direction TRACE's own

The trace line

A single line runs the length of the page like a plotter trace or an audit trail; each section is a plotted point. It begins at a coral origin and ends at a coral terminus — the page is literally a trace.

The name, derived

The masthead spells the acronym out of its own phrase — TRustworthy Artificial intelligenCE — so the wordmark's logic is on the page, not just a slogan over a hero image.

Measurement, not marketing

Content is set like a working paper: shoulder labels, section marks, tabular years, cited venues. Teal from the PI's page carries structure; coral is the one mark; blue stays in the wordmark.
