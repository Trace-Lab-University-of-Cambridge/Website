When can artificial intelligence be trusted in the real world?

A Cambridge laboratory studying how AI is deployed, integrated into decisions, and evaluated in high-stakes settings — healthcare, education, public safety.

§ 01About

We build trustworthy artificial intelligence systems for settings where the cost of error is high. Drawing from machine learning, human–computer interaction, and social science, we investigate when and how AI can be trusted, integrated into human decision-making, and evaluated in ecologically valid conditions — not only on benchmarks, but in the places these systems actually run.
